## 繁體中文

### 宣傳文字
今晚，寫下一句心情，看見附近 5 公里的人。城市太安靜？點一下地球，和東京、首爾、曼谷的夜晚重疊；或走進量子世界，和全世界坐在同一間房間。

### 描述
今晚，你的心情在城市裡發光嗎？

城市低語是一座夜裡的城市。寫下一句此刻的心情，它會化成一顆會呼吸的發光氣泡，浮在你所在的街區。你會看見附近 5 公里內其他人的心情，不用名字、不用照片，只有此刻的感受。

【寫下心情】
一句話就好，不需要手機號碼、Email 或真名。平靜、開心、想念、難過——每種心情都有自己的顏色。

【打卡，才會被看見】
每次打開 App，都由你自己按下「打卡」，附近的人才看得到你；別人看到的是你打卡的地方，不會跟著你移動。不打卡，就沒有人看得到你。

【看見附近，送出微光】
雷達上浮著附近的心情氣泡，只顯示大概的距離，不會顯示地址。雙擊別人的氣泡，送出一道「微光」，讓對方知道有人看見了，不需要開口。

【敲門相遇，十分鐘的對話】
想聊聊，就輕輕敲門。對方同意才會開始對話；對話只有 10 分鐘，有人說話就重新倒數，安靜下來就輕輕結束，訊息從伺服器刪除，不留痕跡。

【低語與低語穿越】
在某個地方留一句話，接下來 7 天，只有經過那一帶的人收得到。也可以寫給一個月後、一年後的自己，回到這裡才打得開。

【平行宇宙】
點一下地球，台灣的城市與東京、首爾、香港、新加坡、吉隆坡、雅加達、胡志明市、曼谷、馬尼拉、雪梨……重疊在一起。兩座城市以各自的中央車站對齊，位置模糊到約 500 公尺，看見遠方此刻的心情，一樣可以敲門聊天。

【量子世界】
全世界的人坐進同一間房間：以台北 101 為原點排成一座蜂巢，你會拿到一個席位代號。沒有人看得到你真正的位置。每個整點，101 原點會響起報時；聊過天的人可以「量子糾纏」，一條光線連著彼此，不管相隔多遠。

【量子化身】
你的大頭貼會變成一顆水晶球，球緣跟著你的心情發光。光球、星環兩款免費，另有選購的彩虹水晶球（一次購買、永久擁有，只是外觀，不影響任何功能）。

【集會】
用一個表情符號當暗號，開一場 5 公里內的活動。把暗號加進稱呼的人就會收到你的公告，整個街區一起亮起來。

【心情據點與城市燈塔】
經過合作的獨立咖啡店，雷達上會亮起一盞燈，走進店裡還能讀到之前坐在這裡的人留下的話。城市燈塔幫你找到附近 2 公里內的性別友善、無障礙與親子廁所。

【點一下翻譯】
對方說日文、韓文、泰文或英文？點一下訊息或心情氣泡就翻成你的語言，翻譯完全在手機上進行。App 支援繁體中文、簡體中文、English、日本語、한국어、Bahasa Melayu、Bahasa Indonesia、Tiếng Việt 與 ไทย。

【城市心情天氣】
今晚的城市，大家多半是什麼心情？匿名統計附近 24 小時內的心情比例。

【安心，是最重要的事】
・僅限 18 歲以上使用
・氣泡、敲門、聊天、低語都能一鍵檢舉與封鎖，封鎖後雙方從彼此的城市消失
・自動過濾不當字詞與電話、網址等聯絡方式
・檢舉在 24 小時內處理
・隨時可以在 App 內永久刪除帳號與所有資料

【點亮之夜】
每週五 21:00–23:00，我們一起上線點亮城市。附近的人越多，城市越亮。

## English

### Promotional text
One line about how you feel tonight, and see who's within 5 km. City too quiet? Overlap with Tokyo, Seoul or Bangkok, or share one room with the world in Quantum World.

### Description
Is your mood glowing in the city tonight?

Mood Whisper is a city at night. Write one line about how you feel right now, and it becomes a softly breathing bubble of light floating over your block. You see the moods of people within 5 km — no names, no photos, just how everyone feels right now.

WRITE YOUR MOOD
One line is enough. No phone number, email or real name. Calm, happy, missing someone, sad — every mood has its own colour.

CHECK IN TO BE SEEN
Every time you open the app, you tap Check in yourself before anyone nearby can see you. Others see the place where you checked in; it never follows you around. No check-in, no one sees you.

SEE WHO'S NEARBY, SEND A GLIMMER
Mood bubbles float on the radar with only a rough distance, never an address. Double-tap someone's bubble to send a glimmer and let them know they were noticed, without saying a word.

KNOCK, THEN TALK FOR TEN MINUTES
Want to talk? Knock gently. The chat starts only if they accept, and it lasts 10 minutes: any new message restarts the countdown, and when you both go quiet it ends softly. Messages are deleted from the server.

WHISPERS AND LETTERS TO THE FUTURE
Leave a line in a place, and for 7 days only people passing by can read it. Or write to yourself a month or a year from now — it opens only when you come back.

PARALLEL UNIVERSE
Tap the globe and Taiwan's cities overlap with Tokyo, Seoul, Hong Kong, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta, Ho Chi Minh City, Bangkok, Manila, Sydney and more. The two cities line up at their central stations, your position is blurred to about 500 m, and you can still knock and chat.

QUANTUM WORLD
Everyone in the world shares one room: a hive around Taipei 101, where you get a seat code. No one sees your real location. Every hour a chime rings at the 101 origin, and people who have chatted can become entangled — a line of light joins your bubbles however far apart you are.

QUANTUM AVATAR
Your photo becomes a crystal orb whose rim glows with your mood. Glow and Ring are free; the optional Rainbow orb is a one-time purchase that's yours forever. It's only a look and doesn't change any feature.

GATHERINGS
Pick one emoji as the secret sign and host an event for everyone within 5 km. People who add it to their name get your announcements, and the whole block lights up together.

MOOD PLACES AND CITY BEACON
Pass a partner independent café and a lamp lights up on your radar; step inside to read what people who sat there before left behind. City Beacon finds gender-friendly, accessible and family restrooms within 2 km.

TAP TO TRANSLATE
Someone writing in Japanese, Korean, Thai or Chinese? Tap a message or mood bubble to translate it into your language — entirely on your phone. Available in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Indonesian, Vietnamese and Thai.

CITY MOOD WEATHER
What's the city feeling tonight? See anonymous mood shares from the last 24 hours nearby.

SAFETY COMES FIRST
• For people 18 and over only
• Report or block from any bubble, knock, chat or whisper; once blocked, you disappear from each other's city
• Offensive words and contact details such as phone numbers and links are filtered automatically
• Reports are handled within 24 hours
• Permanently delete your account and all your data in the app at any time

LIGHT-UP NIGHT
Every Friday from 9 to 11 pm (Taiwan time), we come online together and light up the city. The more people nearby, the brighter it gets.
