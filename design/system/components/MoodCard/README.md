# MoodCard

A shareable card of "how I feel right now", sized for Instagram stories. Drawn at 360×640 and exported at 3× as a 1080×1920 PNG.

- **Top**: eyebrow 「此刻的心情」 in the mood colour (13px, 3px tracking), the mood itself at 30px semibold with a soft glow in the mood colour (up to 4 lines), a 72px glowing rule, then 「2026.10.15 · 夜晚」 and 「在城市的某個角落」 in `ink-muted`.
- **Part of day**: 深夜 before 5, 早晨 before 11, 午後 before 17, 黃昏 before 19, otherwise 夜晚.
- **Bottom**: the night illustration fades up into `night-900`; the gradient wordmark 「城市低語」 sits bottom left with the landing-page address bottom right.
- **No location, ever**: no map, no district, no distance. The card only says 「在城市的某個角落」.
- Opened from the dock's share button, the empty-radar card, or settings. The preview dialog has 「關閉」 (ghost) and 「分享心情卡」; the share text links to the landing page with `?ref=card`.
