Child Safety Standards

App: City Whisper - Mood Whisper (also listed as “Mood Whisper - Nearby Moods” and “城市低語 - Mood Whisper”) · Android package: com.softchip.moodwhisper · Developer: SoftChip · Last updated: November 2026

These standards apply to everyone who uses the City Whisper - Mood Whisper app. SoftChip has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any content or behaviour that sexualises minors are strictly prohibited in the app.

1. Adults 18+ only

  • City Whisper - Mood Whisper is for adults aged 18 and over. Users must confirm their age on first launch; anyone who answers that they are under 18 cannot enter the app.
  • Our Terms of Use set the same age limit. When we find an account that belongs to a minor, we disable it and delete its data.

2. Prohibited content and behaviour

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including images, video, written descriptions or links to such material.
  • Sexualised content involving minors, and any attempt to contact, groom, solicit or arrange to meet a minor.
  • Sharing, requesting, selling or trading sexual content involving minors.
  • Encouraging, praising or giving instructions for any of the above.

3. How we prevent it

  • Content filtering: moods, nicknames and whispers pass through server-side filters, and chat messages through in-app filters, for offensive words and contact details such as phone numbers and links.
  • No photo or video messages: chats are text only, last 10 minutes and are deleted from our servers when they end.
  • Auto-hide: accounts or content reported by several users are hidden automatically until a person reviews them.
  • Human review: every report is reviewed within 24 hours, and child safety reports are handled first.

4. In-app feedback and reporting

Users can raise concerns without leaving the app:

  • Send feedback: Settings › Send feedback opens a form inside the app. Choose “Safety concern”, describe what happened and tap Send. Only the SoftChip team can read it.
  • Report a user or content: long-press any mood bubble, or tap the flag icon on a knock card, in a chat, on a whisper or in an assembly, then choose a reason and add details. You can block the person at the same time; once blocked, you disappear from each other’s city.

5. What we do and who we report to

  • When we confirm a violation, we remove the content immediately, permanently ban the accounts involved, and keep the records needed for an investigation.
  • We report child sexual abuse and exploitation to the authorities as required by law, including the NCMEC CyberTipline, Taiwan’s iWIN and the Taiwan police (110), and to the relevant local and national authorities in the user’s country.
  • We comply with all applicable child safety laws, including Taiwan’s Child and Youth Sexual Exploitation Prevention Act.

6. Child safety contact

SoftChip – City Whisper - Mood Whisper team
Email: service.softchip@gmail.com (please put “Child safety” in the subject).
This contact can explain our CSAM prevention practices and compliance, and responds to child safety reports and to law enforcement.

If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first (110 in Taiwan, 911 in the US).

兒童安全標準

App：城市低語 City Whisper - Mood Whisper · 開發者 SoftChip · 最後更新：2026 年 11 月

本標準適用於所有使用城市低語的人。SoftChip 對兒少性剝削與性虐待（CSAE）採取零容忍。兒少性虐待內容（CSAM）、誘騙兒少（grooming）、性勒索、人口販運，以及任何性化未成年人的內容與行為，在城市低語中一律禁止。

1. 僅限 18 歲以上

  • 城市低語僅限年滿 18 歲的使用者使用。第一次開啟 App 時必須確認年齡，回答未滿 18 歲者無法進入。
  • 使用條款訂有相同的年齡限制；發現未成年使用者時，我們會停用其帳號並刪除資料。

2. 禁止的內容與行為

  • 任何兒少性虐待內容（CSAM），包括照片、影片、文字描述或連結。
  • 以未成年人為對象的性化內容，以及接觸、誘騙、引誘或約見未成年人。
  • 散布、索取、販售或交易涉及未成年人的性內容。
  • 鼓勵、讚揚或教導上述任何行為。

3. 防範措施

  • 內容過濾：心情、暱稱與低語經過伺服器端過濾，聊天訊息在 App 內過濾，濾掉不當字詞與聯絡方式（電話、連結）。
  • 不能傳照片或影片：聊天只有文字，限時 10 分鐘，結束後從伺服器刪除。
  • 自動隱藏：被多位使用者檢舉的帳號或內容會自動隱藏，等待人工審查。
  • 人工審查：所有檢舉在 24 小時內處理；涉及兒少安全的檢舉優先處理。

4. App 內意見回饋與檢舉

  • 意見回饋：設定 ›「意見回饋」會在 App 內打開表單，選「安全疑慮」、寫下經過後按送出，不必離開 App。只有 SoftChip 團隊看得到。
  • 檢舉使用者或內容：長按任何心情氣泡，或在敲門卡片、聊天室、低語、集會中按旗子圖示，選擇原因並補充說明；可以同時封鎖對方，封鎖後雙方從彼此的城市消失。

5. 處置與通報

  • 確認違規時，我們會立即移除內容、永久停用相關帳號，並保留必要紀錄以配合調查。
  • 我們依法向主管機關通報兒少性剝削案件，包括國際的 NCMEC CyberTipline、台灣的 iWIN 網路內容防護機構與警察機關（110），以及使用者所在國家的地方與國家主管機關。
  • 我們遵守所有適用的兒童安全法律，包括《兒童及少年性剝削防制條例》。

6. 兒童安全聯絡窗口

如果有兒童正面臨立即危險，請先撥打 110 報警。
