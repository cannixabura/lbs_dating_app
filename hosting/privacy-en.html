Mood Whisper Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10.8 · Effective October 2026 · Glimmers, city mood weather, whispers and mood trail added October 15, 2026; gatherings added October 21; host hand-over, letters to the future, Taiwan's mood tonight and City Beacon added October 24; Parallel Universe (cities paired by their central stations) and chat translation added, and manual check-in required, October 27; Mood Places and the guestbook added October 28

This policy explains what Mood Whisper (the "Service") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can manage your data.

This policy is a translation of the Traditional Chinese version. If the two versions differ, the Traditional Chinese version prevails.

To keep the community safe, moods, city names, whispers, and gathering names, announcements, questions and answers are automatically filtered for objectionable words.

We do not collect your contacts or photos you did not choose, and we do not get your location when you are not using the app. When you have not checked in, nobody can see your location. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Beta sign-up

The email address, phone type (iPhone or Android), the area you usually spend time in, and how you heard about us, which you enter on the beta sign-up page, are used only to send beta invitations, install links and launch-night notices, and to help us decide which city to launch in first. This information is not linked to your anonymous account in the app and is not shared with third parties, except that iPhone testers' emails are given to Apple TestFlight and Android testers' emails to Google Play so they can send test invitations. It is deleted within one year after the beta ends, or whenever you ask us by email.

2. How long we keep data

  • Location and mood: after you leave the app, kept as a fading echo for 2 hours to 10 days (depending on how many people use the app nearby), then deleted from our servers.
  • Chat messages: deleted when the chat ends.
  • Gatherings: event data, announcements, questions and answers are deleted 30 days after the event ends.
  • Report records: kept for up to 1 year to handle reports and prevent abuse.
  • Other account data: kept until you delete your account in the app or ask us by email to delete it.

3. Third-party services

To provide the Service, your data is processed by the following providers and may be stored outside Taiwan:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, push notifications, cloud functions)
  • Upstash (stores the location index used to find people nearby)
  • Google ML Kit (Android only: translates on your phone; only language models are downloaded, and text is never uploaded)
  • Apple Translation (iPhone only: built into iOS and translates on your phone; text is never uploaded)
  • OpenFreeMap (map tiles; your device downloads maps from it, with no account and no cookies)

4. Your rights

At any time you can: change your mood, city name and profile photo in the app; turn off location or notification permission in your system settings (without location, the radar does not work); delete your account and data immediately in Settings > Delete account and data (report records are kept as described in section 2); or email us to access, correct or delete your data. We handle email requests within 30 days.

5. Children

The Service is only for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we find such data, we delete it immediately.

6. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections. On our servers, authentication and access rules limit each user to reading and writing their own data.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. For significant changes, you will be asked to read and agree again the next time you enter the app.
