# 回覆 App Review：Guideline 1.2（2026-10-25）

送審 1.0.1 (7) 時，在 App Store Connect →「App 審查」的訊息串按「回覆 App 審查」，貼上下面這段英文。

```
Hello App Review team,

Thank you for the feedback. Build 1.0.1 (7) addresses every precaution listed under Guideline 1.2:

1. Age rating 18+: the age rating has been updated to 18+. The app also asks users to confirm they are 18 or older on first launch.
2. Terms (EULA): users must tick a box agreeing to the Terms of Use before entering the city. The terms state that there is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users, and now begin with an English summary: https://mood-whisper-f7de9.web.app/terms.html. The terms version was updated, so every user must agree again.
3. Filtering: moods, nicknames, whispers, gathering announcements and, as of this build, chat messages are filtered for objectionable words and contact details, on the device before sending and again on the server.
4. Flagging: every user bubble (long-press → 檢舉), knock card, chat (flag icon in the header), whisper and gathering announcement has a report option.
5. Blocking: bubble long-press → 封鎖, or "block" in the report sheet. Blocked users disappear from each other's radar and cannot knock or chat.
6. Immediate removal: new in this build, as soon as a report is sent the reported person or post is removed from the reporter's screen immediately and permanently; a reported chat ends at once.
7. 24-hour action: users or posts reported by 3 different people are hidden for everyone automatically. We review every report within 24 hours, remove the offending content and eject the user who posted it.
8. Contact: Settings → 聯絡我們 (Contact us) opens an email to service.softchip@gmail.com. The address is also in the terms and on our website.

To test with demo people, type #review in the mood field on the first screen (details are in the review notes).

Thank you,
Softchip
```
