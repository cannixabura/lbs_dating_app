# EntryCard

The way into the city: one mood line and the terms agreement, under the onboarding headline. Mood only; no nickname on entry.

- Provide the last mood (prefilled for returning people, empty on first launch), the agreement state, and the enter handler.
- The checkbox must be ticked before 走進城市低語 enables, on first launch and whenever the terms change. It names 《使用條款》 and 《隱私權政策》 as links and states zero tolerance for objectionable content (App Store guideline 1.2).
- Empty or blank moods are refused with 「請留下你此刻的心情，再沒入城市。」
- Headline copy: 「準備好相遇了嗎？」 then 「閉上眼睛，感受手機的震動與心跳。現在，寫下你此刻的心情，走進城市低語。」
