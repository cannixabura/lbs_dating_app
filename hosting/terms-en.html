5. Report and block

You can report from the bubble menu, a knock card, a chat, a whisper, a guestbook note or a gathering announcement, and you can block anyone at any time. Once blocked, both people disappear from each other's radar. As soon as you send a report, the reported person or content is removed from your screen (a chat ends immediately). We review every report within 24 hours and, depending on the case, remove the content, hide the account or end it. If one user is reported by 3 different users within 24 hours, the system hides them from the radar until we review it.

Gathering announcements can be reported too. To protect events from bad-faith reports, an announcement is hidden automatically only after enough valid reports: 10 people or 10% of participants, whichever is larger. Only participants present at the time, whose accounts were created before the event started, count as valid. A gathering never ends automatically because of reports. Every report is reviewed by a person within 24 hours, and we may hide the announcement, end the event or end the host's account.

Any user aged 18 or older can host a gathering in the app. It covers 5 km around the host's check-in spot, uses one emoji as its icon, and lasts up to 24 hours. Users within range who have the icon in their city name are participants: they see the host's announcements and receive push notifications.

  • Anonymity. The host cannot see who the participants are. Questions to everyone only show the share of each mood.
  • Hand-over. The host can hand an ongoing event to another participant who is present. Once that person accepts, they become the host and take on the host's responsibilities above. The host chooses whether to notify participants. We keep a record of hand-overs to handle reports.

7. Location and safety

You check in yourself each time you open the app. The Service has no automatic check-in. When you turn on Parallel Universe, or when people in the paired city turn it on, your position appears on their radar blurred and aligned on the two cities' central stations. When you tap Enter on the Quantum World, people there see your seat code and a displayed position around the Taipei 101 origin (your seat plus how far you have walked since entering), never your real location. The Quantum World is not available in mainland China. Translation of chats and moods happens entirely on your phone. Be careful when meeting strangers or sharing personal information. We cannot verify anyone's real identity. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (110 in Taiwan).

9-2. Whisper Groups and the 7-day plan

A Whisper Group is a private group for trips, school outings, hikes, outings or event staff. The person who starts it is the "leader"; everyone else is a "member".

  • Features. The leader can call everyone together, take roll call, set a meeting point and add notes about members. Calls reach members as notifications with a sound and vibration; members who have not tapped "Got it" are reminded once a minute, up to 3 times.
  • Location. Joining a group is not a check-in. The leader cannot see members' locations, only their replies (got it, here, and so on). The meeting point is a place the leader sets; the distance and direction members see are calculated on their own phones.
  • Duration. A group lasts at most 7 days and ends automatically; the leader can also end it early. When a group ends, its member list, roll calls and meeting points are deleted.

The group can then have up to 100 people until it ends. One purchase covers only the group the leader is running, does not renew and is not refunded if the group ends early; to ask for a refund, use Apple's or Google's refund process. Members never pay.
