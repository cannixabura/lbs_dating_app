5. Report and block

You can report from the bubble menu, a knock card, a chat, a whisper or a gathering announcement, and you can block anyone at any time. Once blocked, both people disappear from each other's radar. As soon as you send a report, the reported person or content is removed from your screen (a chat ends immediately). We review every report within 24 hours and, depending on the case, remove the content, hide the account or end it. If one user is reported by 3 different users within 24 hours, the system hides them from the radar until we review it.

Gathering announcements can be reported too. To protect events from bad-faith reports, an announcement is hidden automatically only after enough valid reports: 10 people or 10% of participants, whichever is larger. Only participants present at the time, whose accounts were created before the event started, count as valid. A gathering never ends automatically because of reports. Every report is reviewed by a person within 24 hours, and we may hide the announcement, end the event or end the host's account.

6. Gatherings

Any user aged 18 or older can host a gathering in the app. It covers 5 km around the host's check-in spot, uses one emoji as its icon, and lasts up to 24 hours. Users within range who have the icon in their city name are participants: they see the host's announcements and receive push notifications.

  • The host's responsibility. The host is responsible for the event's name, announcements and questions, and must follow the laws that apply to assemblies, elections and similar activities. Announcements may carry information about the event itself (such as where to meet, the schedule and its cause). They must not be used for commercial advertising, scams or anything prohibited in section 4.
  • Our role. The Service only provides a tool. It is not the organizer or co-organizer of any event, does not check whether an event is lawful, and does not endorse any event, group or political position.
  • Anonymity. The host cannot see who the participants are. Questions to everyone only show the share of each mood.
  • Hand-over. The host can hand an ongoing event to another participant who is present. Once that person accepts, they become the host and take on the host's responsibilities above. The host chooses whether to notify participants. We keep a record of hand-overs to handle reports.
  • Event icon. Only one event at a time can use a given icon in overlapping areas. After an event ends, anyone can use the icon.
  • Leaving. Remove the icon from your city name to leave. After you block the host, you no longer see their announcements or receive their notifications.

7. Location and safety

Only after you tap Check in does the Service show people nearby the spot where you checked in, a rounded distance (such as "within 100 m" or "350 m") and your mood. You check in yourself each time you open the app. The Service has no automatic check-in. When you turn on Parallel Universe, or when people in the paired city turn it on, your position appears on their radar blurred and aligned on the two cities' central stations. Translation of chats and moods happens entirely on your phone. Be careful when meeting strangers or sharing personal information. We cannot verify anyone's real identity. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (110 in Taiwan).
