Mood Whisper (城市低語) is an anonymous, location-based app for adults (18+). No account or password is needed.

New in 1.0.5 and where to find each in-app purchase (all can be tested alone in review mode, below):
- Whisper Groups (private groups for trips): Settings (sliders icon) → 集會 → 開一個低語團 (Start a group). Up to 20 people free. group_pass_7d (consumable) raises this one group to 100 people until it ends: tap 開通 7 天方案 in the leader panel. Meet-up calls use time-sensitive notifications, sent only to members of that group.
- Gifts: tap any person (demo people work) → 送禮物 (Send a gift). gift_bouquet, gift_orb (you pick a style) and gift_pair are consumable so one person can give many people. The recipient accepts or declines; declined or unanswered gifts return to the sender as a voucher. A check before payment blocks gifts the recipient can't receive.
- Quantum avatar (Quantum World → hive button, top right → 量子化身): orb_startrail and orb_rings grow with use (meet cards / time in Quantum World); orb_valentine (until Feb 14) and orb_sakura (until Apr 10) are limited. All non-consumable with Restore Purchases.
- AIYA looks: in the AIYA chat tap ✦ 造型: aiya_skin_xmas (until Dec 25) and aiya_skin_newyear (until Jan 1), non-consumable, visible only to the buyer.
All of these are cosmetic or group tools; none changes matching, visibility or any other user's experience.

Review mode (the radar only shows people within 5 km, so you can test every social feature alone):
1. Skip (略過) the intro, answer 已滿 18 歲 (I am 18 or older) and allow location.
2. Type #review in the mood field, tick the terms box and tap 在這裡打卡，走進城市 (Check in here and enter), then choose 允許 (Allow). This is the manual check-in.
3. 20 demo people (labelled 示範) appear around you. Tap a bubble for Block (封鎖), Report (檢舉), Knock (敲門), Glimmer and 送禮物. A knocked demo person accepts and a 10-minute chat opens.
4. Settings: Block list (封鎖名單), Gifts (禮物), Guide (操作說明), Contact, Delete account.

iPad without GPS: the app uses Wi-Fi positioning, or 在地圖上選擇位置 (Choose on the map).

AIYA (AI chat): in Quantum World tap the 艾婭 · AI label under the light pillar and accept the AI notice. AIYA always says she is an AI; chats last up to 10 minutes; every reply can be reported (long-press). Messages are filtered, and AIYA refuses romance, sexual content, location requests and professional advice. In review mode chats stay free; tap ＋ 時光包 to see time packs (aiya_time_5, aiya_time_20) and AIYA Friend (aiya_friend_monthly).

Location (Guideline 5.1.2(i)):
- The app asks before showing the user on the map (Allow / Don't allow); Settings lets them withdraw at any time.
- Users must tap "Check in here" every time to appear; there is no automatic check-in. The check-in ends when the app goes to the background or the screen locks, and a 打卡中 · 結束打卡 (Check out) button is always on screen.
- Others see the check-in spot, never a live position. Quantum World seats sit in one shared hive around Taipei 101 regardless of real location and are fixed when the user taps Enter.
- Whisper Groups never share members' locations with the leader; 我迷路了 (I'm lost) sends a one-time location only when the member taps it.

User-generated content (Guideline 1.2):
- 18+ rating; users confirm age and accept the terms (zero tolerance for objectionable content): https://mood-whisper-f7de9.web.app/terms-en.html
